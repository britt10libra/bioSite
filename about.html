Her Background

Karen Garner is a hardworking and creative woman who has dedicated much of her life to education. Her career reflects her commitment to helping students learn and succeed.

Her Teaching Career

Karen spent more than three decades working as an English teacher. Although she briefly taught middle school, most of her career was spent teaching high school students, including students attending an alternative continuation high school.

She retired in 2015 and relocated to Georgia. She later returned to education part-time and currently works as a parent liaison at Norton Elementary School in Snellville, Georgia.

What Inspires Her

One of Karen's inspirations was an older teacher nearing retirement who taught at a predominantly Black school. Despite not always receiving a warm welcome, his knowledge and dedication made a lasting impression on her.
